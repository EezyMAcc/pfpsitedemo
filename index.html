A big delivery arrives

Pads, tampons and cups are donated by people, businesses and collection drives. Our volunteers unload them, check them and pack them into boxes.

Off to the venues

Volunteers drive the boxes out to community venues all over Bristol.

3

Venues host a box

Libraries, community centres, places of worship, local shops and food banks keep a Period Friendly box stocked.
